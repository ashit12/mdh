=== Price-level distribution (what a tick ladder must cover) ==================
span is (highest - lowest occupied price + 1) on that side, in ticks; levels is how
many of those ticks hold an order. "in band" is the share of resting orders lying
within a band of that width anchored at the touch and extending away from it.
Worst side of any instrument is reported for the multi-instrument rows.
